# Before and After: User Journeys

Measured on a 375 px-wide phone. A "tap" means any click or tap a visitor has to make. Scrolling is listed separately.

| Visitor | Before (theweightmen.com) | After (prototype) |
|---|---|---|
| **Needs crisis help now** | Header "988 Support" opens 988lifeline.org, then the visitor finds the dial button: **2+ taps, leaves the site**. The homepage Call 988 button is about 12 screens down. | **1 tap**: "Call 988" in the sticky bar dials directly. "Text 988" sits next to it. Works without JavaScript, on every page. |
| **Father going through divorce** | Choose a door → scroll → "Divorce & Starting Again" → **Coming soon** page → "Look for support" → family category: **"Coming soon… Dialing 211 can often point you…"** 4+ taps, ~150 words of reading, no direct action. | Divorce or relationships → Practical help → **LawHelp.org** (legal aid by state), plus the Fatherhood Clearinghouse and 211. **2 taps to a result, 3 to act.** |
| **Recently lost his job** | Choose a door → scroll → Work & Next Steps → "Find a center". 3 taps, ~450 words on the page. | Money or work → Finding work → **American Job Centers** (Call or Website), plus FTC scam checks and 211. **2 + 1 taps.** |
| **Veteran in transition** | Choose a door → Veterans → pick 1 of 4 → action. 3–4 taps, ~390 words. | Military or veteran life → Housing, benefits, or work → **VA homeless call center** (24/7), plus VA.gov and Military OneSource. **2 + 1 taps.** |
| **Lonely** | No door. Relationships is "coming soon". Talk It Through is reachable but not tied to loneliness. | Feeling alone → Reach out to someone I know → **Talk It Through** (the message is written for him), plus 988 and Unload. **2 + 1 taps.** |
| **Grieving** | No door, no resource. Grief appears only in the hero sentence. | Grief or loss → Someone to talk to now → **988**, with the copy "Grief doesn't keep office hours…" Counselor and Unload options also available. **2 + 1 taps.** |
| **Overwhelmed, can't say why** | Must read 10 door descriptions and guess. | I'm not sure → pick talk, write it out, reach out, or practical help → one action. **2 + 1 taps.** |
| **Older phone (320×568)** | Hero, image, and welcome text come first. The first decision is about 2.6 screens down. | The first topic card is visible on the first screen. The crisis bar is one row, 51 px tall. |

| | Before | After |
