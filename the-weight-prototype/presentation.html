How it works

  1. What's weighing on you? Nine plain choices taken from your doors, including “I'm not sure.”
  2. What would help right now? Your Veterans-page question, asked for every topic. It's skipped when the answer is obvious (housing goes straight to 211).
  3. Your next step. One action to do first, up to two alternatives, and what happens when he calls.

Call 988, Text 988, and More help stay at the top of every page, even with JavaScript off.

What changes for the men you're trying to reach

1 tap to dial 988, from any page

2 taps to a specific next step, for every topic

36 words to read before the first choice (was ~133)

0 dead ends: every path ends in a call, a text, a site, or your own tools

Decisions that are yours

  • Six suggested resources (SAMHSA Helpline, FindTreatment.gov, Military OneSource, Fatherhood Clearinghouse, Eldercare Locator, LawHelp.org) fill gaps for grief, counseling, fathers, caregivers, and legal questions. They're marked “not yet checked by The Weight.” Keep, check, or remove them.
  • The FTC job-scams link couldn't be confirmed by automated checks. Worth a manual look.
  • Donations: the About page says no donations right now, but the Support page shows a pay-what-you-can button. Worth aligning.
  • Photography: the prototype reuses your hero image. If you want real photography of men from your community, it drops straight in.

Nothing has been published

This prototype lives in a separate folder. It's marked noindex, says “Design prototype” in the footer, and hasn't been deployed anywhere. Your site is untouched. Every resource sits in one file (assets/js/resources.js), so you can change it in minutes.
